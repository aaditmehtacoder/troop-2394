# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences, weighted equally:

- **Families new to the troop.** Parents of girls and boys in grades 5 to 12 around Santa Clara and Sunnyvale, usually on a phone, deciding whether to come and watch a Tuesday meeting.
- **Current Scouts and families.** Reading the Scouts' stories, checking what is next, heading on to the members' area.

## Product Purpose

The public website of Scouts BSA Troops 394 (boys) and 2394 (girls), run as one linked program under the name Troop 394, chartered by the Santa Clara Elks Lodge in 1993. It shows what the troop actually does, in its own photographs and the Scouts' own words, and turns interest into a visit to a Tuesday meeting.

Success: a new family visits a meeting; a current family finds what they came for in one click.

## Brand Personality

Adventurous, youth-led, welcoming. The Scouts run this troop and the site should sound like it: plain, confident, outdoors. The troop's real photographs carry the page; words are few and each one earns its place.

## Anti-references

- **Busy and wordy.** Many sections, a label and a button on every one, the same information said twice, text where a photograph would do. This describes the home page before the September 2026 redesign.

## Design Principles

1. **Photos first, words last.** A real trip photograph beats a paragraph about it.
2. **Say it once.** No fact, link or call to action repeats across sections.
3. **One clear next step.** Visiting a meeting is the path; everything else is a quiet link.
4. **The Scouts' own voice.** Stories are written by Scouts and credited by first name.
5. **Youth protection is part of the design.** No surnames, no phone numbers, and only photos the troop has cleared (the KindredPix Promotion Folder).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Read mostly on phones and school Chromebooks. Text over photographs keeps AA contrast through overlays, every photograph has real alt text, motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`, and nothing depends on colour alone.
