## Real traditions in the troop's trip log

- **Summer camp: Camp Hi-Sierra**, Long Barn CA (Stanislaus NF), a week every July, six nights. Troop 394 took **first in the camp-wide games two years running**.
- **Elks Lodge Campout & Game Night**, annual, December. Firem'n Chit, pizza, flag retirement, fire to midnight.
- **Wilderness Survival Campout**, annual, late September. Shelter-building from a tarp and sticks, fire-making contest. *Historically held at Camp Pico Blanco, Big Sur. That camp has been **inactive since the 2017 Soberanes Fire**, so the site says "Monterey County coast" instead.*
- **Yosemite**. October.
- **Sequoia & Kings Canyon**. June. Crystal Cave, Moro Rock, General Sherman.
- **Truckee Ski Expedition**. January.
- **Bear Paw snow campout**, near Bear Valley.
- **Pioneer District Camporee**, two nights.
- Overnight bicycle trip over the mountains to the ocean; 25-mile day ride to Lexington Reservoir; kayaking; service at Agnew State Hospital and the Elks Lodge.
- **Quality Unit Award every year** in the troop's early history.
- The troop runs a **Uniform Bank** and a standing **Backpacking Group**.

## Fifteen years of outings, from the troop's own albums

The troop's private KindredPix group (Troops 394 and 2394, group 22) holds
**153 albums spanning 2011 to 2026**. Album titles alone reconstruct the real
programme, and they are the basis for `traditions` and `outingHistory` in
`src/data/troop.ts`.

Traditions this revealed that were not on the site before:

- **Iron Chef**, a patrol cooking competition (2015, 2017, 2019, 2022, 2026)
- **Adopt-a-Campsite**, every year from 2015
- **Street cleanup** for the Elks (2021, 2022, 2025)
- **49ers color guard**, 2019
- **Parade of Champions**, 2022
- **Jamboree on the Air**, 2018
- **Horseback riding campouts** (2020, 2026)
- **River rafting** (2022, 2023) and **kayaking** (Morro Bay 2018, 2023)
- **Pack 32 bridging**, 2021, so Pack 54 is not the only feeder
- Camp Hi-Sierra appears **every single year, 2015 through 2026**

Eagle courts of honor appear in the albums for 2019 (three Scouts), 2021 (four
across two ceremonies), 2022, 2023, 2024 (including a "QUAD Eagle COH"), 2025
(three ceremonies) and 2026. That is a lot more Eagles than we have names for.
